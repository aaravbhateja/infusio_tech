How it works

From first message to a live, automated business

No confusing tech jargon, no disappearing after payment. Five steps, and you know what's happening at each one.

Step 1

Discover

A free call or WhatsApp chat about your business — what's slow, what's manual, where you're losing customers. No pitch, just questions.

Step 2

Propose

You get a plain-language proposal: what we'll build, what it costs, and how long it takes — before any money changes hands.

Step 3

Build

We build in short stages and show you progress along the way, instead of disappearing for weeks and revealing a surprise at the end.

Step 4

Launch

Your site, app, or automation goes live. We walk you (or your staff) through how to use it — no separate manual to decode.

Step 5

Support

We stay reachable after launch for fixes and small changes, and check in monthly on packages that include ongoing management.
